# KavachX - 3-Minute Demo Pitch Script

**Estimated speaking time:** ~2.5 to 3 minutes
**Tone:** Confident, professional, and innovative.

---

### 1. The Hook & Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)
*(Screen showing: Landing Page)*

"Good morning, judges. We are Greybox Labs, and today we’re presenting **KavachX**—a next-generation Cyber Resilience Platform built specifically for modern banking infrastructure. 

In today’s financial sector, security teams aren't lacking data; they are overwhelmed by alert fatigue. They have thousands of alerts but no clear intelligence. KavachX changes that paradigm. It’s not just about collecting alerts; it’s about delivering the *right* intelligence at the *right* moment. Let me show you how."

### 2. Zero-Trust & SOC Dashboard (0:30 - 1:15)
*(Action: Click "Sign In" and transition to the SOC Dashboard)*

"Our platform operates on a strict Zero-Trust architecture. Once logged in, security analysts land on our Command Center—the **SOC Dashboard**. 

Here, you get a real-time, global view of threat vectors. But instead of just showing raw logs, KavachX uses a custom **Threat Forecast Engine**. It analyzes incoming telemetry data—like failed logins, abnormal data transfers, and unusual IP behaviors—and uses Machine Learning to score the risk level of active incidents instantly. It tells the analyst *exactly* what is critical right now, drastically reducing time-to-resolution."

### 3. AI Correlation & AI Copilot (1:15 - 2:00)
*(Action: Navigate to the AI Copilot / Incident view)*

"What truly sets KavachX apart is our **AI Correlation Engine**, powered by Google Gemini. 

When a complex attack happens—say, a VPN login from a new IP followed by database access—it usually takes analysts hours to piece the logs together. KavachX correlates this automatically. 

Even better, we’ve integrated an **AI Security Copilot**. Analysts can literally chat with their telemetry data in real-time. Need a root cause analysis? Just ask the Copilot. Need it explained in a regional language? We support native multilingual translation, including Hindi and Marathi, ensuring security teams across different regional branches can collaborate seamlessly."

### 4. Risk Analytics & What-If Simulator (2:00 - 2:30)
*(Action: Navigate to Risk Analytics / What-If Simulator)*

"We don’t just react to threats; we predict them. 

Using our **What-If Simulator**, CISOs can model potential attack vectors—like a sudden spike in DDoS traffic or a ransomware outbreak—and see exactly how the banking infrastructure would hold up. This allows banks to harden their defenses proactively before a breach ever occurs."

### 5. Compliance & Conclusion (2:30 - 3:00)
*(Action: Navigate to Executive Dashboard)*

"Finally, we know that for banks, security equals compliance. KavachX maps directly to **RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines**, providing automated reporting for executives so they can prove compliance at a glance.

KavachX isn’t just a monitoring tool. It is an intelligent, AI-driven, and RBI-compliant security partner that transforms raw alerts into actionable defense. 

Thank you. We’d love to answer any questions you have."
